Hello, I'm Kamaleshwaran

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology, Coimbatore, with a deep passion for Design Engineering — from precision CAD modelling and structural analysis to full product development and engineering optimization.

My primary strength lies in design: crafting high-performance solutions that are structurally sound, aesthetically refined, and manufacturing-ready. I've led complete kart designs for state-level championships, co-invented 3 filed Indian Patents, and presented research at national conferences.

Beyond core mechanical engineering, I've intentionally built cross-disciplinary skills in coding, embedded systems, IoT, and AI integration — not to switch fields, but to become a more versatile, future-ready engineer. As automation and AI reshape industry, a mechanical engineer who understands intelligent systems can design smarter, more impactful products.
